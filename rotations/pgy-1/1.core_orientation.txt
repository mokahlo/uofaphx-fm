# Core Orientation
**Duration:** 1 month

**Description:** This rotation serves as an introduction to the residency program, focusing on familiarizing residents with the core principles of family medicine, program expectations, and available resources.

**Objectives:**
- Understand the structure and goals of the residency program.
- Meet faculty, staff, and fellow residents.
- Learn about the electronic medical record (EMR) system and clinic workflows.
- Participate in initial training sessions and workshops.
