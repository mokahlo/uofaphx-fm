# Obstetrics Prenatal Clinic
**Duration:** 1 month

**Description:** Focuses on prenatal care, including routine assessments, patient education, and management of common pregnancy-related conditions.

**Objectives:**
- Conduct prenatal visits and assessments.
- Monitor fetal development and maternal health.
- Educate patients on pregnancy, childbirth, and postpartum care.
- Identify and manage common prenatal complications.
