# Underserved  
## Community Health/Underserved – Banner – University Medical Center Phoenix  

**Duration:** 4 weeks  
**Description:** The Community Health/Underserved rotation at Banner – University Medical Center Phoenix (BUMCP) provides family medicine residents with direct experience in addressing healthcare disparities among medically underserved populations in Phoenix. Residents work in community clinics, federally qualified health centers (FQHCs), and public health initiatives, gaining insight into the unique challenges faced by patients with limited access to healthcare. The rotation emphasizes culturally competent care, advocacy, and interdisciplinary collaboration to improve health outcomes.  

**Objectives:**  
- Manage chronic conditions commonly seen in underserved populations, such as diabetes, hypertension, and substance use disorders.  
- Provide preventive care, including vaccinations, cancer screenings, and reproductive health services.  
- Assess the impact of social determinants of health and integrate social services into individualized patient care plans.  
- Collaborate with community health workers, case managers, and behavioral health specialists to address patient needs holistically.  
- Develop skills in motivational interviewing and patient education to improve health literacy and self-care.  
- Advocate for policy changes and initiatives that improve healthcare access and outcomes for underserved populations in Phoenix.  
- Participate in outreach programs and mobile health services to engage with vulnerable populations outside traditional clinical settings.
