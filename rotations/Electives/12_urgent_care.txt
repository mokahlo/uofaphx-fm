# Urgent  
## Urgent Care – Family Medicine Rotation (Banner – University Medical Center Phoenix)  

**Duration:** 4 weeks  
**Description:** The Urgent Care rotation at Banner – University Medical Center Phoenix (BUMCP) provides family medicine residents with experience in the evaluation and management of acute, non-life-threatening conditions commonly encountered in urgent care settings. Residents develop skills in rapid assessment, procedural interventions, and efficient patient care delivery in a fast-paced environment. The rotation emphasizes evidence-based management of minor injuries, infections, and acute illnesses while reinforcing principles of patient safety and appropriate triage.  

**Objectives:**  
- Develop competency in evaluating and managing common urgent care conditions, including respiratory infections, musculoskeletal injuries, and minor lacerations.  
- Gain experience in performing procedures such as wound care, suturing, splinting, incision and drainage, and foreign body removal.  
- Learn to recognize when escalation to emergency care is necessary.  
- Improve efficiency in clinical decision-making and patient throughput in a high-volume setting.  
- Enhance skills in patient education and counseling for self-care and follow-up recommendations.  
- Collaborate with emergency medicine, primary care, and specialty providers to ensure continuity of care.  
- Understand the role of urgent care in healthcare systems, including cost-effective resource utilization and access to care.
