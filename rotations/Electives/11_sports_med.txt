# Sports Med  
## Sports Medicine – Family Medicine Rotation (Banner – University Medical Center Phoenix)  

**Duration:** 4 weeks  
**Description:** The Sports Medicine rotation at Banner – University Medical Center Phoenix (BUMCP) provides family medicine residents with hands-on experience in the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of musculoskeletal injuries and sports-related conditions. Residents work with sports medicine physicians, orthopedic specialists, and physical therapists in outpatient clinics, training rooms, and event coverage settings. The rotation emphasizes non-surgical management, rehabilitation strategies, and injury prevention for athletes and active individuals of all ages.  

**Objectives:**  
- Develop competency in evaluating and managing acute and chronic musculoskeletal injuries.  
- Gain experience in performing joint injections, musculoskeletal ultrasound, and other procedural skills relevant to sports medicine.  
- Understand the principles of rehabilitation, injury prevention, and return-to-play guidelines.  
- Learn to assess and manage concussions and other sports-related head injuries.  
- Collaborate with physical therapists, athletic trainers, and orthopedic specialists in multidisciplinary care.  
- Educate patients on exercise prescription, sports nutrition, and optimizing physical performance.  
- Participate in sideline coverage for sporting events to gain experience in on-field injury management.
