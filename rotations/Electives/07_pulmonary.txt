# Pulmonary  
## Pulmonary 

**Duration:** 4 weeks  
**Description:** The Pulmonary rotation at Banner – University Medical Center Phoenix (BUMCP) provides family medicine residents with comprehensive training in the evaluation and management of common and complex pulmonary conditions. Residents work alongside pulmonologists in both inpatient and outpatient settings, gaining experience in respiratory disease management, pulmonary function testing, and critical care principles. The rotation emphasizes a multidisciplinary approach to lung health, including smoking cessation, chronic disease management, and acute respiratory emergencies.  

**Objectives:**  
- Develop competency in diagnosing and managing common pulmonary conditions, including COPD, asthma, and pneumonia.  
- Gain experience in interpreting pulmonary function tests (PFTs), arterial blood gases (ABGs), and imaging studies.  
- Understand the principles of oxygen therapy, non-invasive ventilation, and advanced airway management.  
- Participate in the management of critically ill patients with respiratory failure and ARDS.  
- Learn to recognize and manage pulmonary embolism, interstitial lung disease, and sleep-disordered breathing.  
- Educate patients on smoking cessation, pulmonary rehabilitation, and medication adherence.  
- Collaborate with respiratory therapists, critical care specialists, and multidisciplinary teams in pulmonary care.
