# Rheumatology  
## Rheumatology – Family Medicine Rotation (Banner – University Medical Center Phoenix)  

**Duration:** 4 weeks  
**Description:** The Rheumatology rotation at Banner – University Medical Center Phoenix (BUMCP) provides family medicine residents with exposure to the diagnosis and management of autoimmune and inflammatory musculoskeletal disorders. Residents work alongside rheumatologists in outpatient clinics, gaining experience in evaluating joint pain, systemic autoimmune diseases, and chronic inflammatory conditions. The rotation emphasizes a multidisciplinary approach, including pharmacologic and non-pharmacologic management strategies.  

**Objectives:**  
- Develop competency in diagnosing and managing common rheumatic diseases, including rheumatoid arthritis, lupus, and gout.  
- Gain experience in performing musculoskeletal exams and recognizing key physical exam findings in autoimmune disorders.  
- Understand the use and interpretation of serologic tests, including ANA, RF, anti-CCP, and inflammatory markers.  
- Learn the indications, mechanisms, and side effects of disease-modifying antirheumatic drugs (DMARDs) and biologic therapies.  
- Collaborate with physical therapists, occupational therapists, and pain management specialists in a comprehensive treatment approach.  
- Educate patients on lifestyle modifications, medication adherence, and the long-term management of chronic rheumatic diseases.  
- Recognize and manage acute rheumatologic emergencies, such as septic arthritis and vasculitis flare-ups.
