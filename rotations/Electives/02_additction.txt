# Addiction  
## Addiction Medicine  

**Duration:** Ongoing throughout a medical professional's career  
**Description:** Addiction medicine is a medical specialty focused on the prevention, diagnosis, treatment, and management of substance use disorders (SUDs) and related behavioral addictions. Physicians specializing in addiction medicine work in a variety of settings, including hospitals, rehabilitation centers, primary care clinics, and research institutions. This field integrates medical, psychological, and social approaches to care, emphasizing harm reduction, recovery support, and public health strategies.  

**Objectives:**  
- Develop expertise in the diagnosis and treatment of substance use disorders, including alcohol, opioid, and stimulant addictions.  
- Gain proficiency in evidence-based interventions such as medication-assisted treatment (MAT), behavioral therapies, and harm reduction strategies.  
- Understand the neurobiology of addiction and its impact on physical and mental health.  
- Foster interdisciplinary collaboration with mental health professionals, social workers, and community organizations to provide comprehensive patient care.  
- Advocate for policies that support addiction prevention, treatment access, and public health initiatives.  
- Engage in research and continuing education to stay updated on emerging treatments and best practices in addiction medicine.
