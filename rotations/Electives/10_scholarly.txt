# Scholarly  
## Scholarly Activity/Project Month – Family Medicine Rotation (Banner – University Medical Center Phoenix)  

**Duration:** 4 weeks  
**Description:** The Scholarly Activity/Project Month at Banner – University Medical Center Phoenix (BUMCP) provides family medicine residents with dedicated time to engage in academic research, quality improvement initiatives, or community-based projects. This rotation allows residents to develop their skills in research methodology, data analysis, and scholarly writing while contributing to advancements in medical knowledge, education, or patient care. Residents work closely with faculty mentors to complete a project that aligns with their interests and career goals.  

**Objectives:**  
- Develop and complete a scholarly project in clinical research, quality improvement, medical education, or community health.  
- Gain experience in research methodology, data collection, and statistical analysis.  
- Learn to critically appraise medical literature and apply evidence-based medicine principles.  
- Prepare a manuscript, abstract, or presentation suitable for publication or conference submission.  
- Collaborate with faculty mentors and interdisciplinary teams to refine research skills.  
- Understand the ethical considerations and institutional review board (IRB) process in medical research.  
- Present findings to peers, faculty, or at regional/national conferences.
