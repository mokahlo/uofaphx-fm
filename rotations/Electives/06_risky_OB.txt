# Risky OB  
## High-Risk Obstetrics – Family Medicine Rotation (Banner – University Medical Center Phoenix)  

**Duration:** 4 weeks  
**Description:** The High-Risk Obstetrics rotation at Banner – University Medical Center Phoenix (BUMCP) provides family medicine residents with advanced training in the management of complex pregnancies. Residents work alongside obstetricians, maternal-fetal medicine specialists, and neonatal care teams to develop skills in identifying and managing obstetric complications. The rotation emphasizes evidence-based prenatal care, intrapartum management, and postpartum care for high-risk patients.  

**Objectives:**  
- Develop competency in managing high-risk pregnancies, including preeclampsia, gestational diabetes, and multiple gestations.  
- Gain experience in fetal monitoring, obstetric ultrasound, and emergency obstetric interventions.  
- Understand the indications for and collaborate in the management of preterm labor and preterm birth prevention.  
- Work alongside maternal-fetal medicine specialists to co-manage complex cases.  
- Provide counseling and education to patients on risk factors, lifestyle modifications, and perinatal complications.  
- Assist in labor and delivery, including vaginal and cesarean births in high-risk scenarios.  
- Recognize and manage postpartum complications, including hemorrhage and hypertensive disorders.
