# GI  
## GI/Proctology

**Duration:** 2-4 weeks (varies by program)  
**Description:** The GI/Proctology rotation in family medicine provides residents with hands-on experience in diagnosing and managing common gastrointestinal and anorectal conditions encountered in primary care. The rotation emphasizes outpatient care, preventive screenings, and interdisciplinary collaboration with gastroenterologists and colorectal specialists. Residents gain exposure to evidence-based approaches for treating conditions such as hemorrhoids, irritable bowel syndrome (IBS), gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD), and colorectal cancer screening.  

**Objectives:**  
- Develop competency in evaluating and managing common GI complaints, including abdominal pain, constipation, diarrhea, and reflux.  
- Gain proficiency in conducting focused GI physical exams and interpreting diagnostic tests such as stool studies and imaging.  
- Learn indications for and counsel patients on colorectal cancer screening methods, including FIT, colonoscopy, and sigmoidoscopy.  
- Understand the management of common anorectal disorders such as hemorrhoids, anal fissures, and rectal prolapse.  
- Collaborate with specialists to coordinate care for patients with inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) and functional GI disorders.  
- Educate patients on lifestyle modifications, dietary changes, and medication management for chronic GI conditions.
