# Global Health  
## Global Health/International – Family Medicine Rotation (Banner – University Medical Center Phoenix)  

**Duration:** 4 weeks  
**Description:** The Global Health/International rotation at Banner – University Medical Center Phoenix (BUMCP) provides residents with exposure to healthcare challenges and medical practices in international and resource-limited settings. Through clinical experiences, public health initiatives, and global health policy discussions, residents gain insight into healthcare disparities, infectious disease management, and cross-cultural medicine. This rotation emphasizes sustainable healthcare solutions and the role of family physicians in global health efforts.  

**Objectives:**  
- Develop competency in managing infectious diseases prevalent in global health settings, such as tuberculosis, malaria, and HIV.  
- Gain experience in providing primary and preventive care in resource-limited environments.  
- Understand the impact of social, economic, and environmental factors on global health disparities.  
- Collaborate with international healthcare teams, NGOs, and public health organizations.  
- Learn about global health policies, humanitarian aid efforts, and ethical considerations in international medicine.  
- Improve cross-cultural communication and adaptability in diverse healthcare settings.  
- Explore sustainable healthcare models and strategies for capacity building in underserved regions.
