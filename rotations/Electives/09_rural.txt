# Rural  
## Rural

**Duration:** 4 weeks  
**Description:** The Rural rotation at Banner – University Medical Center Phoenix (BUMCP) provides family medicine residents with firsthand experience in delivering comprehensive healthcare in rural and medically underserved areas. This rotation emphasizes primary care in resource-limited settings, requiring residents to develop broad clinical skills across multiple disciplines, including emergency medicine, obstetrics, pediatrics, and chronic disease management. Residents work in rural clinics and hospitals, collaborating with local healthcare providers to address unique challenges in rural medicine.  

**Objectives:**  
- Develop competency in managing a wide range of medical conditions with limited access to specialty care.  
- Gain experience in providing emergency and urgent care in rural settings.  
- Understand the challenges of healthcare delivery in rural areas, including transportation barriers and provider shortages.  
- Collaborate with rural healthcare teams, including nurse practitioners, physician assistants, and community health workers.  
- Enhance procedural skills relevant to rural practice, such as wound care, joint injections, and point-of-care ultrasound.  
- Educate patients on preventive care, chronic disease management, and health literacy in underserved communities.  
- Advocate for policies and programs that improve healthcare access and resources in rural areas.
