# Peds
## Pediatrics - Banner University Medical Center

**Duration:** 1 month  
**Description:** Residents will gain experience in the comprehensive care of pediatric patients, focusing on well-child visits, acute illness management, and chronic disease care. The rotation includes exposure to both inpatient and outpatient pediatric medicine, emphasizing developmental monitoring and preventive health strategies.  

**Objectives:**  
- Perform age-appropriate physical exams and developmental screenings for pediatric patients.  
- Diagnose and manage common pediatric conditions, including respiratory infections, asthma, otitis media, and gastroenteritis.  
- Provide anticipatory guidance on nutrition, immunizations, and childhood safety.  
- Recognize and manage pediatric emergencies such as dehydration, febrile seizures, and anaphylaxis.  
- Gain proficiency in pediatric procedures, including vaccine administration, IV placement, and lumbar punctures.  
- Interpret pediatric-specific laboratory values, imaging studies, and growth charts.  
- Collaborate with pediatric subspecialists, social workers, and child life specialists to optimize patient care.  
- Develop strong communication skills for interacting with children and their families, addressing concerns in a compassionate and developmentally appropriate manner.  
- Participate in inpatient rounds, outpatient clinics, and newborn care to gain a broad perspective on pediatric medicine.
