# Urology
## Urology

**Duration:** 1 month  
**Description:** Residents will gain experience in the evaluation, diagnosis, and management of urologic conditions affecting both male and female patients. The rotation includes exposure to outpatient urology clinics, inpatient consultations, and surgical procedures.  

**Objectives:**  
- Perform comprehensive urologic evaluations, including history-taking and focused genitourinary exams.  
- Diagnose and manage common urologic conditions such as urinary tract infections, kidney stones, benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH), and erectile dysfunction.  
- Interpret imaging studies, including renal ultrasound, CT scans, and urodynamic testing.  
- Assist in urologic procedures such as cystoscopy, bladder catheterization, and prostate biopsies.  
- Recognize and manage urologic emergencies, including testicular torsion, acute urinary retention, and obstructing nephrolithiasis.  
- Gain experience in perioperative care for urologic surgeries, including transurethral resection of the prostate (TURP) and nephrectomy.  
- Work collaboratively with urologists, nephrologists, and oncologists in the management of complex urologic conditions.  
- Counsel patients on preventive measures and lifestyle modifications for urologic health.  
- Participate in both outpatient and inpatient urology services to gain a well-rounded perspective on the specialty.
