# Ortho
## Musculoskeletal/Orthopedics

**Duration:** 1 month  
**Description:** Residents will gain experience in the evaluation, diagnosis, and management of musculoskeletal injuries and conditions. The rotation focuses on non-surgical and surgical approaches to orthopedic care, emphasizing physical examination skills, imaging interpretation, and procedural techniques.  

**Objectives:**  
- Perform thorough musculoskeletal exams to assess joint, ligament, tendon, and bone injuries.  
- Interpret imaging studies such as X-rays, MRIs, and ultrasounds for orthopedic conditions.  
- Develop non-surgical management plans, including physical therapy, bracing, and injections.  
- Assist in orthopedic procedures such as joint aspirations, fracture reductions, and casting/splinting.  
- Recognize and manage common orthopedic emergencies, including open fractures, compartment syndrome, and dislocations.  
- Gain experience in the preoperative and postoperative care of orthopedic surgery patients.  
- Work collaboratively with physical therapists, sports medicine specialists, and surgeons to optimize patient recovery.  
- Provide patient education on injury prevention, rehabilitation, and long-term musculoskeletal health.  
- Participate in outpatient orthopedic clinics and inpatient consults to gain a broad perspective on musculoskeletal care.
