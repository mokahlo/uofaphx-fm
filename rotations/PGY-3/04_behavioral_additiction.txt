# Addiction
## Behavioral Health/Addiction Medicine - Banner University Medical Center

**Duration:** 1 month  
**Description:** Residents will gain experience in the evaluation and treatment of substance use disorders, focusing on harm reduction, medication-assisted treatment (MAT), and behavioral therapy interventions. The rotation emphasizes a multidisciplinary approach to addiction medicine, integrating psychiatry, primary care, and social services.  

**Objectives:**  
- Perform comprehensive assessments of patients with substance use disorders, including screening, diagnosis, and withdrawal management.  
- Develop proficiency in the use of medication-assisted treatment (MAT), including buprenorphine, methadone, and naltrexone.  
- Recognize and manage withdrawal syndromes associated with alcohol, opioids, and benzodiazepines.  
- Learn counseling techniques such as motivational interviewing and cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) for addiction.  
- Collaborate with social workers, case managers, and rehabilitation programs to coordinate patient care and referrals.  
- Gain experience in managing co-occurring psychiatric disorders, including depression, anxiety, and PTSD in patients with addiction.  
- Understand harm reduction strategies, including needle exchange programs and naloxone distribution.  
- Educate patients and families on addiction as a chronic disease and available treatment options.  
- Participate in inpatient and outpatient addiction medicine consults and detox protocols.
