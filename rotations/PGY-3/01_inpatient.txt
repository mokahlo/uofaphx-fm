# FM Service
## Family Medicine Service - Banner University Medical Center

**Duration:** 1 month  
**Description:** Residents will gain experience in the management of hospitalized and outpatient family medicine patients, emphasizing continuity of care, interdisciplinary collaboration, and comprehensive patient-centered management. The rotation includes exposure to acute and chronic disease management across a broad spectrum of conditions.  

**Objectives:**  
- Perform thorough history-taking and physical examinations for hospitalized and outpatient family medicine patients.  
- Develop and implement evidence-based management plans for common acute and chronic conditions such as hypertension, diabetes, COPD, and infections.  
- Gain experience in inpatient care, including daily rounding, discharge planning, and care coordination.  
- Manage acute presentations in the outpatient setting, including respiratory illnesses, musculoskeletal injuries, and dermatologic conditions.  
- Collaborate with specialists, case managers, and social workers to ensure comprehensive patient care.  
- Interpret diagnostic tests, including laboratory studies, imaging, and ECGs, to guide patient management.  
- Develop proficiency in common office-based procedures such as joint injections, skin biopsies, and contraceptive placement.  
- Educate patients and families about preventive care, lifestyle modifications, and chronic disease management.  
- Understand and implement transitions of care from inpatient to outpatient settings.
