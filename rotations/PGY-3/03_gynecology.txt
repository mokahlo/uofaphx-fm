# GYN
## Gynecology - Banner University Medical Center

**Duration:** 1 month  
**Description:** Residents will gain experience in the evaluation and management of gynecologic conditions, including routine well-woman exams, contraception counseling, and the diagnosis and treatment of gynecologic disorders. The rotation includes both outpatient and inpatient care, as well as exposure to surgical procedures.  

**Objectives:**  
- Perform comprehensive gynecologic evaluations, including Pap smears, breast exams, and STI screenings.  
- Develop proficiency in contraception counseling and family planning, including IUD and implant insertions.  
- Diagnose and manage common gynecologic conditions such as abnormal uterine bleeding, PCOS, and pelvic pain.  
- Assist in gynecologic surgeries, including hysterectomies, laparoscopic procedures, and colposcopies.  
- Gain experience in the evaluation and treatment of acute gynecologic emergencies, such as ectopic pregnancy and ovarian torsion.  
- Interpret diagnostic imaging, including pelvic ultrasounds, to guide patient management.  
- Provide patient education on menopause, hormone replacement therapy, and reproductive health.  
- Work collaboratively with obstetricians, oncologists, and primary care providers in managing complex gynecologic conditions.  
- Participate in preoperative and postoperative care for gynecologic patients.
