# Emergency
## Emergency Medicine

**Duration:** 1 month  
**Description:** Residents will gain hands-on experience in the assessment, stabilization, and management of acutely ill and injured patients in a high-paced emergency department setting. The rotation emphasizes rapid decision-making, procedural skills, and interdisciplinary teamwork.  

**Objectives:**  
- Perform initial evaluations and resuscitations for critically ill patients.  
- Develop proficiency in airway management, including intubation and non-invasive ventilation.  
- Learn to recognize and manage common medical and surgical emergencies.  
- Gain experience in performing emergency procedures, such as central lines, lumbar punctures, and chest tube placements.  
- Work collaboratively with trauma teams, consultants, and nursing staff to optimize patient care.  
- Improve efficiency in patient flow and decision-making in a fast-paced environment.
