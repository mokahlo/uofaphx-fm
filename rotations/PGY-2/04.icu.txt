# ICU at BUMCP
**Duration:** 1 month

**Description:** 
Residents will gain experience in managing critically ill patients in a high-acuity intensive care unit.

**Objectives:**
- Perform and interpret critical care monitoring.
- Manage ventilators and vasoactive medications.
- Participate in rapid response and code blue situations.
- Develop an approach to end-of-life and palliative care discussions.
