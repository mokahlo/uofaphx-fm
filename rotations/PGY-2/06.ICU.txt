# ICU
## ICU

**Duration:** 1 month  
**Description:** Residents will gain hands-on experience in the management of critically ill patients, focusing on advanced monitoring, resuscitation, and ventilatory support. The rotation emphasizes multidisciplinary collaboration, rapid decision-making, and mastery of invasive procedures.  

**Objectives:**  
- Perform comprehensive assessments of critically ill patients, including hemodynamic monitoring and ventilator management.  
- Gain proficiency in airway management, including endotracheal intubation and non-invasive ventilation.  
- Learn to manage shock states, including sepsis, cardiogenic shock, and hemorrhagic shock.  
- Develop skills in performing critical care procedures such as central line placement, arterial line insertion, and chest tube placement.  
- Interpret arterial blood gases (ABGs), ECGs, and advanced imaging relevant to ICU patients.  
- Work closely with a multidisciplinary team, including intensivists, respiratory therapists, and nursing staff, to optimize patient outcomes.  
- Participate in daily rounds, family discussions, and end-of-life care planning.
