# Ortho
## Musculoskeletal and Sports Medicine - Banner University Medical Center

**Duration:** 1 month  
**Description:** Residents will focus on the diagnosis, treatment, and rehabilitation of musculoskeletal injuries and sports-related conditions. The rotation emphasizes hands-on physical examination skills, imaging interpretation, and non-surgical management of orthopedic injuries.  

**Objectives:**  
- Perform comprehensive musculoskeletal exams to assess joint, ligament, tendon, and bone injuries.  
- Interpret imaging studies such as X-rays, MRIs, and ultrasounds for common musculoskeletal conditions.  
- Develop non-surgical management plans, including physical therapy, bracing, and injections.  
- Participate in sports team coverage and gain experience in event medicine.  
- Learn procedures such as joint aspirations, cortisone injections, and ultrasound-guided therapies.  
- Collaborate with orthopedic surgeons, physical therapists, and athletic trainers to optimize patient outcomes.
