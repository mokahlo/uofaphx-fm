# Ortho
## Musculoskeletal/Sports Medicine	
**Duration:** 1 month

**Description:** 
This rotation provides training in acute care and emergency medicine at BUMCP's emergency department.

**Objectives:**
- Triage and manage high-acuity emergency presentations.
- Perform emergency procedures (intubation, chest tube placement).
- Recognize and treat life-threatening conditions.
- Work efficiently in a fast-paced emergency setting.
