# Ambulatory
## Family Medicine Ambulatory Care - Banner University Medical Center

**Duration:** 1 month  
**Description:** Residents will develop skills in the outpatient management of acute and chronic conditions, preventive care, and patient-centered primary care. The rotation emphasizes continuity of care, evidence-based management, and effective communication with patients and families.  

**Objectives:**  
- Provide comprehensive primary care for patients across all age groups.  
- Manage common chronic conditions, including hypertension, diabetes, and hyperlipidemia.  
- Perform preventive screenings and vaccinations based on current guidelines.  
- Develop proficiency in office-based procedures such as joint injections, skin biopsies, and contraceptive management.  
- Improve patient counseling skills for lifestyle modifications and chronic disease management.  
- Coordinate care with specialists, social workers, and other healthcare providers.  
- Enhance efficiency in outpatient workflow, including documentation and time management.
