# Ambulatory
## Family Medicine Ambulatory Care

**Description:** 
This rotation focuses on managing acutely ill adults in an inpatient setting, emphasizing evidence-based decision-making.

**Objectives:**
- Perform comprehensive history-taking and physical exams.
- Develop differential diagnoses and treatment plans.
- Manage multi-system disease processes in hospitalized patients.
- Collaborate with subspecialists and allied health professionals.
