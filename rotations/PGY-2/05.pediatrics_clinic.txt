# Pediatrics Clinic
**Duration:** 1 month

**Description:** 
This rotation focuses on outpatient pediatric care, covering newborns to adolescents.

**Objectives:**
- Perform well-child visits and vaccinations.
- Diagnose and manage common pediatric illnesses.
- Provide anticipatory guidance and parental education.
- Recognize red flags for referral to pediatric subspecialties.
