# Derm
## Dermatology

**Duration:** 1 month

**Description:** 
This rotation emphasizes the care of elderly patients, including polypharmacy management, functional assessment, and end-of-life care.

**Objectives:**
- Conduct comprehensive geriatric assessments.
- Recognize and manage cognitive impairment and dementia.
- Address palliative care needs and advanced care planning.
- Manage common geriatric syndromes such as falls and frailty.
