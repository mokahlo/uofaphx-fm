# OB Labor and Delivery
**Duration:** 1 month

**Description:** 
This rotation provides hands-on training in managing laboring patients, deliveries, and postpartum care.

**Objectives:**
- Monitor labor progression and fetal well-being.
- Assist with spontaneous vaginal deliveries.
- Manage obstetric emergencies such as shoulder dystocia and postpartum hemorrhage.
- Provide postpartum counseling and lactation support.
