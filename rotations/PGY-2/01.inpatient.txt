# Inpatient
## Family Medicine Inpatient	

**Duration:** 6 weeks

**Description:** 
This rotation focuses on outpatient care in the FM clinic, emphasizing continuity of care, chronic disease management, and preventive medicine.

**Objectives:**
- Conduct comprehensive primary care visits.
- Manage chronic diseases such as diabetes and hypertension.
- Develop patient communication and shared decision-making skills.
- Perform in-office procedures under supervision.
