# Inpatient
## Family Medicine Inpatient - Banner University Medical Center

**Duration:** 1 month  
**Description:** Residents will gain experience in the management of hospitalized patients with acute and chronic medical conditions. The rotation emphasizes clinical reasoning, evidence-based medicine, and interdisciplinary collaboration in an inpatient setting.  

**Objectives:**  
- Perform comprehensive history and physical examinations on hospitalized patients.  
- Develop differential diagnoses and management plans for common inpatient conditions.  
- Manage acute exacerbations of chronic diseases such as heart failure, COPD, and diabetes.  
- Interpret laboratory results, imaging studies, and bedside procedures to guide patient care.  
- Gain proficiency in inpatient procedures, including central line placement, lumbar punctures, and paracentesis.  
- Collaborate with a multidisciplinary team, including hospitalists, pharmacists, case managers, and social workers.  
- Participate in daily rounds, discharge planning, and care coordination to optimize patient outcomes.
