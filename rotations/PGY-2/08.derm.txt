# Derma
## Dermatology - Banner University Medical Center

**Duration:** 1 month  
**Description:** Residents will gain exposure to the diagnosis and management of common and complex dermatologic conditions. The rotation includes experience in outpatient clinics, dermatologic procedures, and inpatient consults. Emphasis is placed on recognizing dermatologic manifestations of systemic diseases and developing treatment plans.  

**Objectives:**  
- Perform comprehensive skin examinations to diagnose common and rare dermatologic conditions.  
- Learn to differentiate benign, pre-malignant, and malignant skin lesions.  
- Develop proficiency in using dermoscopy for enhanced skin lesion evaluation.  
- Gain experience in dermatologic procedures, including biopsies, cryotherapy, excisions, and intralesional injections.  
- Recognize dermatologic emergencies, such as Stevens-Johnson Syndrome and toxic epidermal necrolysis.  
- Understand the role of systemic therapies, biologics, and immunosuppressive agents in dermatology.  
- Work collaboratively with other specialties to manage dermatologic manifestations of systemic diseases.
