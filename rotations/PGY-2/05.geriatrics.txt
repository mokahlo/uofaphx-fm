# Geriatrics
## Geriatrics

**Duration:** 1 month

**Description:** 
Residents will manage hospitalized pediatric patients at Phoenix Children's Hospital (PCH), gaining exposure to a wide range of pediatric conditions.

**Objectives:**
- Work within a pediatric team to manage acutely ill children.
- Develop skills in fluid and medication dosing for pediatric patients.
- Perform neonatal resuscitation and pediatric procedures.
- Participate in family-centered rounds.
