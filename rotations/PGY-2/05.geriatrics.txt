# Geriatrics
## Geriatrics

**Duration:** 1 month  
**Description:** Residents will focus on the comprehensive care of older adults, including the management of chronic diseases, polypharmacy, cognitive impairment, and end-of-life planning. The rotation emphasizes a multidisciplinary approach to geriatric syndromes and functional assessment.  

**Objectives:**  
- Perform comprehensive geriatric assessments, including functional, cognitive, and psychosocial evaluations.  
- Learn to manage common geriatric syndromes such as frailty, falls, delirium, and incontinence.  
- Develop expertise in deprescribing and managing polypharmacy to reduce adverse drug effects.  
- Gain experience in palliative care and end-of-life discussions, including advanced care planning.  
- Recognize and address elder abuse and neglect.  
- Collaborate with a multidisciplinary team, including physical therapists, occupational therapists, social workers, and palliative care specialists.  
- Understand transitions of care, including discharge planning, home health services, and long-term care placement.
