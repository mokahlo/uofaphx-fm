# Peds Emergency
## Pediatric Emergency Medicine - Phoenix Children's Hospital

**Duration:** 1 month   
**Description:** Residents will focus on musculoskeletal injuries and rehabilitation strategies for athletes and active individuals.    

**Objectives:**
- Perform comprehensive musculoskeletal exams.
- Interpret imaging for common sports injuries.
- Develop non-surgical management plans for orthopedic conditions.
- Participate in sports team coverage and event medicine.
