# Peds Emergency
## Pediatric Emergency Medicine - Phoenix Children's Hospital

**Duration:** 1 month  
**Description:** Residents will gain experience in the evaluation and management of acutely ill and injured pediatric patients in an emergency department setting. The rotation focuses on rapid assessment, procedural skills, and the unique aspects of pediatric emergency care.  

**Objectives:**  
- Perform focused pediatric assessments for acute medical and traumatic conditions.  
- Develop proficiency in pediatric airway management, including intubation and non-invasive ventilation.  
- Recognize and manage pediatric-specific emergencies such as bronchiolitis, febrile seizures, and anaphylaxis.  
- Gain experience in pediatric procedures, including IV placement, lumbar punctures, and fracture reductions.  
- Understand pediatric dosing for medications and fluid resuscitation.  
- Work collaboratively with pediatric subspecialists, trauma teams, and child life specialists to optimize patient care.  
- Improve communication skills for interacting with pediatric patients and their families in high-stress situations.
