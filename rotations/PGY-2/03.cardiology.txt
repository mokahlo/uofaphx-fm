# Cardio
## Cardiology - Banner University Medical Center

**Duration:** 1 month  
**Description:** Residents will gain experience in the diagnosis and management of cardiovascular diseases, including coronary artery disease, heart failure, arrhythmias, and valvular disorders. The rotation emphasizes clinical decision-making, ECG interpretation, and cardiovascular risk assessment.  

**Objectives:**  
- Perform comprehensive cardiovascular evaluations, including history-taking and physical exams.  
- Interpret ECGs, echocardiograms, and stress tests to diagnose cardiac conditions.  
- Learn to manage common cardiac conditions such as hypertension, heart failure, and arrhythmias.  
- Gain experience in inpatient and outpatient cardiology consults.  
- Assist in procedures such as cardiac catheterization, echocardiography, and stress testing.  
- Understand indications and contraindications for advanced cardiac interventions, including PCI and CABG.  
- Work collaboratively with cardiologists, interventionalists, and electrophysiologists in patient management.
