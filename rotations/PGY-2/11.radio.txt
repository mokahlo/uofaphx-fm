# Radiology
## Diagnostic Radiology - Banner University Medical Center

**Duration:** 1 month  
**Description:** Residents will gain exposure to a broad range of imaging modalities, including X-ray, CT, MRI, and ultrasound. The focus is on image interpretation, radiologic-pathologic correlation, and communication of findings to referring providers.  

**Objectives:**  
- Develop proficiency in interpreting common imaging studies, including X-ray, CT, MRI, and ultrasound.  
- Learn to recognize emergent and critical findings requiring immediate intervention.  
- Understand appropriate imaging modalities for different clinical scenarios.  
- Improve communication skills through interactions with referring physicians and multidisciplinary teams.  
- Participate in daily case reviews, image-guided procedures, and didactic lectures.
