# Underserved
## Community Medicine - Banner University Medical Center

**Duration:** 1 month  
**Description:** Residents will focus on providing healthcare to underserved populations, addressing social determinants of health, and improving access to care. The rotation emphasizes cultural competency, community outreach, and resource navigation for vulnerable patient populations.  

**Objectives:**  
- Provide primary care to underserved populations, including uninsured and underinsured patients.  
- Identify and address social determinants of health that impact patient outcomes.  
- Gain experience in managing common chronic diseases in resource-limited settings.  
- Learn to navigate community resources, including housing assistance, food programs, and transportation services.  
- Develop cultural competency in caring for diverse populations.  
- Participate in mobile clinics, free clinics, and community outreach programs.  
- Work alongside social workers, case managers, and public health professionals to provide holistic care.
