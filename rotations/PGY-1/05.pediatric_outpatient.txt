# Peds Outpatient at Valleywise
**Duration:** 1 month

**Description:** Focuses on ambulatory pediatric care, including preventive health, acute illness management, and growth and development monitoring.

**Objectives:**
- Perform well-child checks and vaccinations.
- Manage common outpatient pediatric conditions.
- Educate families on health maintenance and disease prevention.
- Coordinate care with community resources.
