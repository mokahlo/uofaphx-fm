# Peds Outpatient
## Pediatrics Outpatient at Valleywise - Valleywise Health

**Duration:** 1 month  
**Description:** Residents will develop skills in the outpatient management of common pediatric conditions, preventive care, and patient-centered medical home principles. The rotation focuses on well-child visits, acute care, and chronic disease management in an ambulatory setting.  

**Objectives:**  
- Conduct well-child visits, including developmental screenings, growth monitoring, and immunizations.  
- Diagnose and manage common pediatric outpatient conditions such as upper respiratory infections, otitis media, and gastroenteritis.  
- Provide anticipatory guidance and education to parents on nutrition, safety, and developmental milestones.  
- Recognize and manage chronic pediatric conditions, including asthma, obesity, and ADHD.  
- Gain experience in office-based pediatric procedures such as vision and hearing screening, vaccine administration, and minor wound care.  
- Develop strong communication skills to address parental concerns and educate families on pediatric health topics.  
- Coordinate care with pediatric subspecialists, school health programs, and social services when needed.  
- Participate in quality improvement initiatives aimed at enhancing pediatric outpatient care.
