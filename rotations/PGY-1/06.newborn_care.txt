# Newborn
## Newborn Care - Valleywise Health

**Duration:** 1 month  
**Description:** Residents will gain hands-on experience in the care of healthy and high-risk newborns in a hospital setting. The rotation focuses on neonatal assessment, stabilization, feeding, and preventive care in the immediate postpartum period.  

**Objectives:**  
- Perform comprehensive newborn examinations, including APGAR scoring and gestational age assessment.  
- Recognize and manage common neonatal conditions such as jaundice, hypoglycemia, and respiratory distress.  
- Provide education to parents on breastfeeding, safe sleep practices, and newborn care.  
- Learn neonatal resuscitation techniques and participate in high-risk deliveries.  
- Monitor weight gain, feeding patterns, and thermoregulation in newborns.  
- Interpret newborn screening tests and ensure appropriate follow-up for abnormal results.  
- Administer routine newborn vaccinations and prophylactic medications (vitamin K, erythromycin eye ointment, and hepatitis B vaccine).  
- Coordinate care with lactation consultants, social workers, and pediatric specialists when necessary.  
- Understand hospital discharge criteria for healthy newborns and provide anticipatory guidance to families.
