# Newborn Care at Valleywise
**Duration:** 1 month

**Description:** Provides experience in the care of newborns, including routine assessments and management of common neonatal conditions.

**Objectives:**
- Perform newborn physical examinations.
- Manage conditions like jaundice and feeding difficulties.
- Educate parents on newborn care and safety.
- Collaborate with lactation consultants and pediatricians.
