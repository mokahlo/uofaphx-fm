# Peds Inpatient at PCH
## Peds Inpatient at PCH
**Duration:** 1 month

**Description:** This rotation offers experience in managing hospitalized children with diverse medical conditions, enhancing pediatric assessment and treatment skills.

**Objectives:**
- Conduct pediatric histories and physical exams.
- Manage common pediatric illnesses.
- Work with pediatric specialists and support staff.
- Participate in family-centered rounds.
