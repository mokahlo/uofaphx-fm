# Peds Inpatient
## Pediatrics Inpatient - Phoenix Children's Hospital

**Duration:** 1 month  
**Description:** Residents will gain hands-on experience in managing hospitalized pediatric patients with acute and chronic medical conditions. The rotation emphasizes clinical assessment, evidence-based management, and coordination of care with pediatric subspecialists.  

**Objectives:**  
- Perform comprehensive history and physical examinations on pediatric inpatients.  
- Develop differential diagnoses and management plans for common pediatric conditions such as respiratory infections, dehydration, and neonatal jaundice.  
- Recognize and manage pediatric emergencies, including sepsis, status asthmaticus, and diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA).  
- Interpret pediatric-specific laboratory tests, imaging studies, and bedside procedures.  
- Gain proficiency in inpatient procedures such as IV placement, lumbar punctures, and nasogastric tube insertion.  
- Collaborate with a multidisciplinary team, including pediatric subspecialists, nurses, case managers, and child life specialists.  
- Participate in daily rounds, discharge planning, and care coordination to optimize patient outcomes.  
- Develop communication skills for discussing diagnoses, treatment plans, and prognosis with parents and guardians.  
- Understand the principles of pediatric medication dosing and fluid management.
