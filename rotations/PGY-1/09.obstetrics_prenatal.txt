# OB
## Prenatal Clinic - Banner University Medical Center

**Duration:** 1 month  
**Description:** Residents will gain experience in the comprehensive management of pregnant patients, focusing on prenatal care, risk assessment, and maternal-fetal health monitoring. The rotation emphasizes early identification of complications, patient education, and multidisciplinary collaboration.  

**Objectives:**  
- Perform comprehensive prenatal visits, including history-taking, physical exams, and fetal monitoring.  
- Assess and manage common pregnancy-related conditions such as gestational diabetes, hypertension, and anemia.  
- Provide patient education on nutrition, exercise, and expected physiologic changes during pregnancy.  
- Interpret obstetric ultrasound findings and fetal heart rate tracings.  
- Counsel patients on prenatal screening tests and genetic counseling options.  
- Monitor and document fetal growth, amniotic fluid levels, and maternal well-being.  
- Recognize warning signs for preterm labor, preeclampsia, and fetal distress.  
- Work collaboratively with obstetricians, maternal-fetal medicine specialists, and social workers to optimize prenatal care.  
- Develop a plan for labor and delivery, including birth preferences and risk assessments.
