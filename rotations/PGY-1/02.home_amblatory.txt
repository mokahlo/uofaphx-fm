# Ambulatory
## Family Medicine Home Ambulatory

**Duration:** 1 month  
**Description:** Residents will engage in providing comprehensive primary care to patients in their homes, focusing on managing chronic conditions, preventive care, and addressing social determinants of health. This rotation emphasizes patient-centered care, continuity, and the effective delivery of healthcare services in a home setting.  

**Objectives:**  
- Conduct thorough medical histories and physical examinations in patients' homes, adapting to the home environment to provide effective care.  
- Manage acute and chronic medical conditions commonly encountered in the home setting, including hypertension, diabetes, and mobility issues.  
- Develop and implement individualized care plans that consider the patient's home environment, resources, and support systems.  
- Identify and address social determinants of health that impact patient outcomes, such as housing conditions, access to nutritious food, and social support.  
- Educate patients and their families on disease management, medication adherence, and lifestyle modifications to promote health and prevent disease progression.  
- Collaborate with interdisciplinary teams, including nurses, social workers, and physical therapists, to provide holistic care and coordinate services.  
- Document patient encounters accurately and efficiently, utilizing portable electronic medical records systems when available.  
- Understand the logistics and challenges of delivering healthcare in the home setting, including safety considerations and resource limitations.  
- Participate in quality improvement initiatives aimed at enhancing the delivery of home-based primary care services.
