# Labor and Delivery
## Labor and Delivery at BUMCP

**Duration:** 1 month

**Description:** Involves managing laboring patients, performing deliveries, and providing postpartum care.

**Objectives:**
- Conduct labor assessments and manage labor progression.
- Perform vaginal deliveries and assist in cesarean sections.
- Provide postpartum care and education.
- Recognize and manage obstetric emergencies.
