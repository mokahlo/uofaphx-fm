# Labor and Delivery
## Labor and Delivery

**Duration:** 1 month  
**Description:** Residents will gain hands-on experience in the management of labor, delivery, and postpartum care. The rotation emphasizes intrapartum monitoring, obstetric procedures, and collaboration with multidisciplinary teams to ensure optimal maternal and fetal outcomes.  

**Objectives:**  
- Perform and document labor assessments, including cervical exams, fetal heart monitoring interpretation, and labor progression tracking.  
- Assist in vaginal deliveries, operative deliveries, and cesarean sections.  
- Recognize and manage common obstetric complications such as preeclampsia, postpartum hemorrhage, and fetal distress.  
- Develop proficiency in obstetric procedures, including amniotomy, fetal scalp electrode placement, and intrauterine pressure catheter insertion.  
- Participate in neonatal resuscitation and immediate postpartum care of newborns.  
- Counsel patients on labor pain management options, including epidural anesthesia and non-pharmacologic techniques.  
- Work collaboratively with obstetricians, midwives, anesthesiologists, and nursing staff to ensure safe and effective labor management.  
- Gain experience in postpartum rounds, including breastfeeding support and discharge planning for mother and baby.  
- Understand and implement evidence-based guidelines for the induction and augmentation of labor.
